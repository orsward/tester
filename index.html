Reusable Pack Structure

  • 20 track titles per niche.
  • One short repeatable chorus hook.
  • Lyrics template using [Verse]/[Chorus] tags.
  • Single structured Suno style prompt.
  • YouTube + Spotify metadata fields and artwork prompt.
Sample compliance checklist
  • Use unique ISRC per track.
  • Keep metadata consistent but not generic/repetitive.
  • Disclose synthetic/AI-generated content on YouTube where required.
  • Vary covers, durations, and descriptions across releases.

Production Workflow

  1. Pick one niche per release cycle.
  2. Generate 20 tracks with a shared sonic identity.
  3. Use unique titles, artwork, and descriptions.
  4. Keep chorus simple and brandable.
  5. Export track-level metadata + unique ISRCs.
  6. Upload in small waves.
  7. Track performance by niche, title pattern, and playlist fit.
